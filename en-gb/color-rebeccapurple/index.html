Using Rebecca Purple well

Rebecca Purple sits in the purple / violet family at 40% lightness — a mid-tone, moderately saturated, cool colour. As a background it pairs best with white text (8.41:1 contrast). Its complement (#669933) is the high-energy partner for calls-to-action and highlights; the analogous neighbours (#333399, #993399) give calm, same-family gradients. For print, start from the CMYK values above and proof — screen Rebecca Purple is an sRGB colour and rich screens shades can shift on press.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the hex code for Rebecca Purple?

#663399 — the CSS named colour “rebeccapurple”. Its RGB is (102, 51, 153).

Is Rebecca Purple good for text or backgrounds?

As a background, white text reads best on it (8.41:1). As text itself on white it reaches 8.41:1 — enough for WCAG AA normal text.

What colours go with Rebecca Purple?

Its complement #669933 for contrast, analogous #333399 and #993399 for calm combinations, and the 50–950 shade scale above for monochrome designs.

What is Rebecca Purple in CMYK for printing?

C33 M67 Y0 K40. CMYK conversions of screen colours are a starting point — always proof before a print run.
