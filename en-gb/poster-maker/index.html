Poster & Flyer Maker — Free A4, Square & Story Poster Designer

In short: the Poster / Flyer Maker is a small canvas studio: pick a ready size (A4 ratio, square post, story, landscape or portrait feed), start from a template, then drag text and shape blocks anywhere, restyle them, and export a print- or post-ready PNG.

School events, shop sales, community announcements and social posts all need a poster that looks designed, not typed. Start from a template, swap in your words, drag things into place — and share or print within minutes.

Tip: click a block to select it, drag to move it, drag its bottom-right corner to resize.
Private by design: everything in this tool happens 100% in your browser with JavaScript. Your data, photos and designs are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.

How to use the Poster / Flyer Maker

  1. Choose your poster size — A4 for printing, Square or Portrait for social feeds, Story for full-screen stories, Landscape for screens and banners.
  2. Load a starter template (Big Sale, Community Event or Quote) or begin with Add text and Add rectangle / circle.
  3. Click any block to select it; drag to move it, drag its bottom-right corner to resize, and use the editor below the canvas for text, size, colour, alignment and exact dimensions.
  4. Optionally set a background colour or upload a background photo, then press Download poster PNG.

How it works

The poster is a stack of blocks — text blocks with font size, colour, bold and alignment, and shape blocks — rendered onto a canvas at the exact pixel size you picked, so the export is ready to post or print without further resizing. A background photo is cover-fitted to the whole canvas. Selection, dragging and corner-resizing are handled with pointer and touch events, and blocks are clamped so they can never be dragged off the canvas.

Worked example

A shop owner picks the Square size and the Big Sale template, changes the headline to "Winter Clearance", moves the gold circle behind the words "SHOP NOW", and downloads a 1080 × 1080 PNG that goes straight onto the shop page and WhatsApp status.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which size should I print?

Choose A4 portrait — it exports at the A4 ratio (794 × 1123 px), suitable for home and shop printing. For social media, use Square (posts) or Story (full-screen).

Can I use my own photo as the background?

Yes — upload it with the Background image picker. It is cover-fitted to the poster and stays on your device.

How do I move or resize a block?

Click the block to select it, drag it to move, and drag the small square at its bottom-right corner to resize. Exact width and height can also be typed in the editor.

Is the poster maker free?

Yes — completely free, no sign-up, no watermark, and your design never leaves your browser.

Can I save my poster to edit later?

Download the PNG for the finished poster. The editor keeps your work while the tab stays open; for a new design, reload a template and adjust.
