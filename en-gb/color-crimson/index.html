Using Crimson well

Crimson sits in the red family at 47% lightness — a mid-tone, vivid, warm colour. As a background it pairs best with white text (4.99:1 contrast). Its complement (#14DCB4) is the high-energy partner for calls-to-action and highlights; the analogous neighbours (#DC14A0, #DC5014) give calm, same-family gradients. For print, start from the CMYK values above and proof — screen Crimson is an sRGB colour and rich screens shades can shift on press.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the hex code for Crimson?

#DC143C — the CSS named colour “crimson”. Its RGB is (220, 20, 60).

Is Crimson good for text or backgrounds?

As a background, white text reads best on it (4.99:1). As text itself on white it reaches 4.99:1 — enough for WCAG AA normal text.

What colours go with Crimson?

Its complement #14DCB4 for contrast, analogous #DC14A0 and #DC5014 for calm combinations, and the 50–950 shade scale above for monochrome designs.

What is Crimson in CMYK for printing?

C0 M91 Y73 K14. CMYK conversions of screen colours are a starting point — always proof before a print run.
