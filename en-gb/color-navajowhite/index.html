Using Navajowhite well

Navajowhite sits in the orange family at 84% lightness — a light, vivid, warm colour. As a background it pairs best with black text (16.30:1 contrast). Its complement (#ADCEFF) is the high-energy partner for calls-to-action and highlights; the analogous neighbours (#FFB5AD, #F7FFAD) give calm, same-family gradients. For print, start from the CMYK values above and proof — screen Navajowhite is an sRGB colour and rich screens shades can shift on press.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the hex code for Navajowhite?

#FFDEAD — the CSS named colour “navajowhite”. Its RGB is (255, 222, 173).

Is Navajowhite good for text or backgrounds?

As a background, black text reads best on it (16.30:1). As text itself on white it reaches 1.29:1 — not enough for body text at AA; use it for headings, large text or accents.

What colours go with Navajowhite?

Its complement #ADCEFF for contrast, analogous #FFB5AD and #F7FFAD for calm combinations, and the 50–950 shade scale above for monochrome designs.

What is Navajowhite in CMYK for printing?

C0 M13 Y32 K0. CMYK conversions of screen colours are a starting point — always proof before a print run.
