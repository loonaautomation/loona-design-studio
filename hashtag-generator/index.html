Hashtag Generator — Free Hashtags That Get You Found

In short: the Hashtag Generator builds a balanced set for your post in seconds: a mix of popular hashtags for reach, medium hashtags for steady discovery and niche hashtags where your post can actually stand out — for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn and X.

One giant hashtag gives you a second of fame in a flood; one tiny hashtag gives you loyal viewers who were looking for exactly what you made. The sets here blend all three sizes, so every post gets reach and a real chance to be seen.

Platform tip:

Tip: click any hashtag chip to remove it before copying.

Private by design: everything in this tool happens 100% in your browser with JavaScript. Your choices are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us — close the tab and everything is gone.

How to use the Hashtag Generator

  1. Pick the platform you are posting on — each one shows a short tip on how its hashtags work best.
  2. Pick your topic and how many hashtags you want: 10, 20 or 30.
  3. Press Generate hashtags. You get a balanced mix of popular, medium and niche hashtags with no duplicates.
  4. Click any chip to remove a hashtag you do not want, press Regenerate for a fresh mix, then Copy all and paste the set into your post.

How it works

Every set is drawn from a hand-picked bank for your topic in three tiers: popular hashtags (roughly 40%) for reach, medium hashtags (roughly 35%) for steady discovery, and niche hashtags (roughly 25%) where smaller accounts can rank. The mix is shuffled at random on every Generate and Regenerate, duplicates are impossible, and the hashtags themselves are the same in every language edition — only this page's text is translated.

Worked example

A food blogger posting a street-food reel picks Instagram + Food + 20: the set pairs big reach tags like #foodie with niche tags a small account can win, and she removes two chips that do not fit the dish before copying.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hashtags should I use?

It depends on the platform: Instagram and TikTok reward 3–5 well-chosen hashtags, LinkedIn 3–5, X just 1–2, and YouTube shows the first 3 above your title. Generate a full set here, then trim it with the platform tip shown for your choice.

Why mix popular, medium and niche hashtags?

Popular hashtags bring reach but bury small posts in seconds. Niche hashtags have loyal audiences searching for exactly your topic. A blend gives every post both a big door and a door it can actually walk through.

Can I remove hashtags I do not like?

Yes — click any hashtag chip to remove it. The text block updates instantly, and Copy all copies only the hashtags that remain.

Is it free?

Yes — free, private, no sign-up and no watermark. Everything runs in your browser.
