Business Card Maker — Free Print-Ready Cards at 300 DPI

In short: the Business Card Maker designs a true print-size card — 3.5 × 2 inches at 300 DPI, exactly 1050 × 600 pixels — with your name, title, phone, email, website and address, a logo icon, three layouts and five colour schemes, exported as a print-ready PNG.

A business card is often the first designed thing a customer ever holds. Poster makers use screen sizes; this maker works in real card dimensions, so what you download is what the print shop needs — sharp, correctly proportioned and ready to print.

Private by design: everything in this tool happens 100% in your browser with JavaScript. Your data, photos and designs are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.

How to use the Business Card Maker

  1. Fill in your name, title, business name and contact details — the card updates as you type.
  2. Choose a layout: Side band (classic), Top band (centered) or Minimal, then pick a colour scheme and a logo icon.
  3. Check every detail carefully — spelling of your name and number matters most on a printed card.
  4. Download the PNG (1050 × 600 px, 300 DPI size) and send it to any print shop, or print it on card stock at exactly 3.5 × 2 inches.

How it works

The canvas is locked to 1050 × 600 pixels — the exact pixel count of a 3.5 × 2 inch card at the print standard of 300 dots per inch. Long names are auto-fitted: the maker estimates the rendered text width and reduces the font until the name fits its line, so nothing ever overflows the card edge. Layouts are drawn with the same icon glyphs as the Logo Maker for a matching brand look.

Worked example

A freelance tutor enters her name, "Home Tuition — Maths & Science", her phone and the Loona site she books through, picks the Clean Blue top-band layout, and downloads the card. The print shop produces 200 cards from that single file with no resizing or blur.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size will my card print?

Exactly 3.5 × 2 inches — the international standard business card size — because the file is 1050 × 600 pixels at 300 DPI.

Why not design my card in the Poster Maker?

The Poster Maker is for posters and social graphics at screen sizes. This maker is locked to true card dimensions and print resolution, with layouts designed for card proportions.

Can I print the back of the card too?

Design a second card the same way (for example, Minimal layout with just your services) and ask your print shop to use it as the back.

Will my name fit if it is long?

Yes — the name font auto-shrinks to fit its line. Very long names stay on one line and fully inside the card.

Is the business card maker free?

Yes — free, no sign-up, no watermark; your details are typed and rendered only in your browser.
