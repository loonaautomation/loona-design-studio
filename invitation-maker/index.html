Invitation Maker — Free Invitations (Birthday, Wedding, Events)

In short: the Invitation Maker designs graceful invitation cards — names, occasion, date, venue and RSVP — at shareable portrait size and a printable A5 ratio, from two editable templates.

An invitation sets the tone before the event begins. These templates hold the classic structure — occasion, hosts, date, place — so you only supply the joyful details.

Click a block to select it, drag to move, drag its bottom-right corner to resize. Layers: use Send backward / Bring forward.

Private by design: everything in this tool happens 100% in your browser with JavaScript. Your data, photos and designs are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.

How to use the Invitation Maker

  1. Pick a size and a starter template — every template is fully editable, nothing is locked.
  2. Click any text, shape or photo block to edit it: drag to move, drag the corner to resize, restyle in the editor below the canvas.
  3. Add your own photo blocks and background, arrange layers with Send backward / Bring forward.
  4. Download the PNG at the exact size you picked, or use Print / Save as PDF for paper copies.

How it works

Portrait size is made for WhatsApp and social sharing — how most invitations travel today. The A5 ratio prints two invitations per A4 sheet at home. Keep venue and phone double-checked: an invitation’s only job is accurate joy. This page runs on Loona’s shared canvas editor: your design is a stack of text, shape and photo blocks rendered at the exact pixel size chosen, so exports are print- and platform-ready. Blocks are clamped inside the canvas and layers can be reordered. Everything happens in your browser — photos are never uploaded.

Worked example

A family designs the birthday invitation in fifteen minutes and sends it to forty guests on WhatsApp — no printing, no courier, everyone informed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I print invitations?

Choose the A5 ratio and Print / Save as PDF — two fit on one A4 sheet.

Is there a wedding template?

Yes — the elegant Wedding template with space for families’ names; edit every line.

Can I add a photo of the birthday child?

Yes — + Add photo block places it anywhere on the card.

Is it free?

Yes — free, private, watermark-free.
