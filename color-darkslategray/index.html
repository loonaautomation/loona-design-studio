Using Dark Slate Gray well

Dark Slate Gray sits in the cyan / teal family at 25% lightness — a dark, muted, cool colour. As a background it pairs best with white text (8.93:1 contrast). Its complement (#4F2F2F) is the high-energy partner for calls-to-action and highlights; the analogous neighbours (#2F4F3F, #2F3F4F) give calm, same-family gradients. For print, start from the CMYK values above and proof — screen Dark Slate Gray is an sRGB colour and rich screens shades can shift on press.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the hex code for Dark Slate Gray?

#2F4F4F — the CSS named colour “darkslategray”. Its RGB is (47, 79, 79).

Is Dark Slate Gray good for text or backgrounds?

As a background, white text reads best on it (8.93:1). As text itself on white it reaches 8.93:1 — enough for WCAG AA normal text.

What colours go with Dark Slate Gray?

Its complement #4F2F2F for contrast, analogous #2F4F3F and #2F3F4F for calm combinations, and the 50–950 shade scale above for monochrome designs.

What is Dark Slate Gray in CMYK for printing?

C41 M0 Y0 K69. CMYK conversions of screen colours are a starting point — always proof before a print run.
