Using Darkviolet well

Darkviolet sits in the purple / violet family at 41% lightness — a mid-tone, vivid, cool color. As a background it pairs best with white text (6.56:1 contrast). Its complement (#3FD300) is the high-energy partner for calls-to-action and highlights; the analogous neighbors (#2A00D3, #D300A8) give calm, same-family gradients. For print, start from the CMYK values above and proof — screen Darkviolet is an sRGB color and rich screens shades can shift on press.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the hex code for Darkviolet?

#9400D3 — the CSS named color “darkviolet”. Its RGB is (148, 0, 211).

Is Darkviolet good for text or backgrounds?

As a background, white text reads best on it (6.56:1). As text itself on white it reaches 6.56:1 — enough for WCAG AA normal text.

What colors go with Darkviolet?

Its complement #3FD300 for contrast, analogous #2A00D3 and #D300A8 for calm combinations, and the 50–950 shade scale above for monochrome designs.

What is Darkviolet in CMYK for printing?

C30 M100 Y0 K17. CMYK conversions of screen colors are a starting point — always proof before a print run.
