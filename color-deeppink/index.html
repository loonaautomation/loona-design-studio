Using Deeppink well

Deeppink sits in the magenta / pink family at 54% lightness — a mid-tone, vivid, cool colour. As a background it pairs best with black text (5.77:1 contrast). Its complement (#14FF80) is the high-energy partner for calls-to-action and highlights; the analogous neighbours (#F614FF, #FF141D) give calm, same-family gradients. For print, start from the CMYK values above and proof — screen Deeppink is an sRGB colour and rich screens shades can shift on press.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the hex code for Deeppink?

#FF1493 — the CSS named colour “deeppink”. Its RGB is (255, 20, 147).

Is Deeppink good for text or backgrounds?

As a background, black text reads best on it (5.77:1). As text itself on white it reaches 3.64:1 — not enough for body text at AA; use it for headings, large text or accents.

What colours go with Deeppink?

Its complement #14FF80 for contrast, analogous #F614FF and #FF141D for calm combinations, and the 50–950 shade scale above for monochrome designs.

What is Deeppink in CMYK for printing?

C0 M92 Y42 K0. CMYK conversions of screen colours are a starting point — always proof before a print run.
