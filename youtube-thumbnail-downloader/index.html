YouTube Thumbnail Downloader — Free, in Every Size

In short: paste any YouTube video link and download its thumbnail in every size YouTube really holds — HD 1280 × 720 when the uploader added one, plus SD, HQ, MQ and the small default. Missing sizes are marked honestly, never upscaled and faked.

Every public YouTube video already has its thumbnail stored on YouTube's own image servers, in up to five fixed sizes. This tool finds the video's ID in any link you paste, loads each size, checks what actually came back, and hands you clean JPG downloads — no sign-up, no watermark, no fake HD.

Private by design: images load straight from YouTube's public image CDN (i.ytimg.com) into your browser; nothing is uploaded to our servers, the link you paste is never stored or seen by us, and there is no sign-up. Close the tab and everything is gone.

How to use the YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

  1. Copy the video's link from YouTube — a watch link, a youtu.be short link, a Shorts, Live or embed link all work — or copy just its 11-character video ID.
  2. Paste it into the box above and press Get thumbnails.
  3. Check the preview cards: each one shows the size that really exists for this video. A size YouTube does not hold is marked “Not available for this video”, never faked.
  4. Press Download on the size you want and the JPG saves straight to your device, or use View full size to open the original in a new tab.
  5. Need the video itself? Copy its watch link or its Shorts link from the list under the results.

How it works

YouTube stores every public video's still images on its public image CDN at fixed addresses: i.ytimg.com/vi/<video ID>/<size>.jpg. The five sizes are maxresdefault (1280 × 720), sddefault (640 × 480), hqdefault (480 × 360), mqdefault (320 × 180) and default (120 × 90). This tool reads the video ID out of whatever link format you paste, builds those five addresses, and loads each image in your browser. The important detail: when a size does not exist, YouTube does not return an error — it quietly returns a tiny 120 × 90 placeholder image instead. So the tool measures the real pixel size of every image that loads, and any “big” size that arrives as 120 × 90 is treated as what it is: not available. If even hqdefault comes back as the placeholder, the ID does not resolve to a public video and the tool tells you instead of showing broken pictures.

Worked example

Paste the link to “Me at the zoo” (video ID jNQXAC9IVRw), the first video ever uploaded to YouTube. It predates HD thumbnails, so its maxresdefault and sddefault addresses return only the 120 × 90 placeholder — both cards are marked “Not available for this video” — while hqdefault (480 × 360), mqdefault (320 × 180) and default (120 × 90) are real and downloadable. A modern upload such as Big Buck Bunny (aqz-KE-bpKQ) has all five sizes, including the full HD maxresdefault. Same tool, two honest answers — because the answer depends on the video, not on us.

Honest about HD — no fake upscaling

The full HD 1280×720 thumbnail exists only when the uploader added one. Some videos only have the smaller sizes YouTube generated automatically — when that happens we tell you and show what really exists. We never upscale a small image and call it HD.

Thumbnails belong to their creators — download them for reference, study or your own channel work, and respect the creator's rights.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download a YouTube thumbnail?

Paste the video link (or its 11-character video ID) into the box and press Get thumbnails. Every size YouTube holds for that video appears with a preview — press Download on the size you want and the JPG saves straight to your device.

Why is the HD 1280×720 size missing for some videos?

The full HD 1280×720 thumbnail exists only when the uploader added one. Some videos only have the smaller sizes YouTube generated automatically — when that happens we tell you and show what really exists. We never upscale a small image and call it HD.

Which link formats work?

Watch links, youtu.be short links, Shorts, Live and embed links, Music and mobile links, links without https://, and a bare 11-character video ID — anything that carries the video's ID.

Is it free, and do I need an account?

Yes — completely free, with no sign-up and no watermark. The images load straight from YouTube's public image CDN; nothing you paste is uploaded to or stored on our servers.

Can I reuse a thumbnail I download?

Thumbnails belong to their creators — download them for reference, study or your own channel work, and respect the creator's rights. Reusing someone else's thumbnail as your own can breach copyright and YouTube's policies.
