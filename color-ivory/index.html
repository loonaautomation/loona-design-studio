Using Ivory well

Ivory sits in the near-white / pastel family at 97% lightness — a light, vivid, warm colour. As a background it pairs best with black text (20.81:1 contrast). Its complement (#F0F0FF) is the high-energy partner for calls-to-action and highlights; the analogous neighbours (#FFF8F0, #F8FFF0) give calm, same-family gradients. For print, start from the CMYK values above and proof — screen Ivory is an sRGB colour and rich screens shades can shift on press.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the hex code for Ivory?

#FFFFF0 — the CSS named colour “ivory”. Its RGB is (255, 255, 240).

Is Ivory good for text or backgrounds?

As a background, black text reads best on it (20.81:1). As text itself on white it reaches 1.01:1 — not enough for body text at AA; use it for headings, large text or accents.

What colours go with Ivory?

Its complement #F0F0FF for contrast, analogous #FFF8F0 and #F8FFF0 for calm combinations, and the 50–950 shade scale above for monochrome designs.

What is Ivory in CMYK for printing?

C0 M0 Y6 K0. CMYK conversions of screen colours are a starting point — always proof before a print run.
