Using Orchid well

Orchid sits in the magenta / pink family at 65% lightness — a mid-tone, moderately saturated, cool colour. As a background it pairs best with black text (7.27:1 contrast). Its complement (#70DA74) is the high-energy partner for calls-to-action and highlights; the analogous neighbours (#A970DA, #DA70A1) give calm, same-family gradients. For print, start from the CMYK values above and proof — screen Orchid is an sRGB colour and rich screens shades can shift on press.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the hex code for Orchid?

#DA70D6 — the CSS named colour “orchid”. Its RGB is (218, 112, 214).

Is Orchid good for text or backgrounds?

As a background, black text reads best on it (7.27:1). As text itself on white it reaches 2.89:1 — not enough for body text at AA; use it for headings, large text or accents.

What colours go with Orchid?

Its complement #70DA74 for contrast, analogous #A970DA and #DA70A1 for calm combinations, and the 50–950 shade scale above for monochrome designs.

What is Orchid in CMYK for printing?

C0 M49 Y2 K15. CMYK conversions of screen colours are a starting point — always proof before a print run.
