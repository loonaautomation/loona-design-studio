Using White well

White sits in the neutral (grey family) family at 100% lightness — a light, muted, neutral colour. As a background it pairs best with black text (21.00:1 contrast). Its complement (#FFFFFF) is the high-energy partner for calls-to-action and highlights; the analogous neighbours (#FFFFFF, #FFFFFF) give calm, same-family gradients. For print, start from the CMYK values above and proof — screen White is an sRGB colour and rich screens shades can shift on press.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the hex code for White?

#FFFFFF — the CSS named colour “white”. Its RGB is (255, 255, 255).

Is White good for text or backgrounds?

As a background, black text reads best on it (21.00:1). As text itself on white it reaches 1.00:1 — not enough for body text at AA; use it for headings, large text or accents.

What colours go with White?

Its complement #FFFFFF for contrast, analogous #FFFFFF and #FFFFFF for calm combinations, and the 50–950 shade scale above for monochrome designs.

What is White in CMYK for printing?

C0 M0 Y0 K0. CMYK conversions of screen colours are a starting point — always proof before a print run.
