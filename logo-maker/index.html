Logo Maker — Free Logo Design with Icons, Fonts & Transparent PNG

In short: the Logo Maker designs a clean, original logo from your brand name and tagline — six font styles (including a script wordmark), eight colour schemes, ten built-in icons and three layouts — and exports it as a transparent or coloured PNG at 512 or 1024 pixels.

A new shop, channel, class project or side business needs a logo long before it needs a design agency. Type your name, try the schemes and icons, and download a tidy logo today — then refine it whenever your brand grows.

Note: this maker creates original brand artwork for your business or project. It is different from the document Signature Generator in Loona PDF Tools, which is for signing documents.

Private by design: everything in this tool happens 100% in your browser with JavaScript. Your data, photos and designs are never uploaded to a server, never stored and never seen by us - close the tab and everything is gone.

How to use the Logo Maker

  1. Type your brand name and an optional tagline.
  2. Choose a layout: icon left for websites and cards, icon on top for social profiles, or the circular badge for stickers and stamps.
  3. Pick an icon and a font style — the script wordmark gives an elegant signature-style brand mark (this is brand artwork, not a document signature).
  4. Tap a colour scheme or fine-tune the icon and text colours, choose Transparent background for overlays, and download at 512 or 1024 px.

How it works

The icons are original simple glyphs drawn as vector paths directly onto the canvas, so they stay razor sharp at any export size. Text is auto-fitted: the maker shrinks the font until your full brand name fits its layout width. With the transparent option, no background is painted at all, so the PNG drops cleanly onto photos, videos and documents.

Worked example

A home baker names her business "Loona Blooms", picks the leaf icon, the Classic Serif font and the Teal & Gold scheme, and exports a transparent 1024-pixel badge logo — ready for her Instagram profile, box stickers and invoice header the same evening.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this logo maker really free?

Yes — free with no sign-up and no watermark. The logo you make is yours to use for your business or project.

Can I get a transparent logo?

Yes — set Background to Transparent and download. The PNG keeps only the icon and text, ideal over photos and coloured backgrounds.

What is the difference from a signature generator?

This maker creates brand artwork — a logo for a business or channel. For signing PDF documents, use the Signature Generator in Loona PDF Tools instead.

Which export size should I choose?

512 px is plenty for profile pictures and website headers. Choose 1024 px for print, stickers or large banners.

Can I use my logo commercially?

The designs are generated from original built-in glyphs and standard font stacks for your own brand use. For a registered trademark, have the final artwork checked by a professional.
