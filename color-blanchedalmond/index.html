Using Blanchedalmond well

Blanchedalmond sits in the orange family at 90% lightness — a light, vivid, warm colour. As a background it pairs best with black text (18.02:1 contrast). Its complement (#CDE1FF) is the high-energy partner for calls-to-action and highlights; the analogous neighbours (#FFD2CD, #FAFFCD) give calm, same-family gradients. For print, start from the CMYK values above and proof — screen Blanchedalmond is an sRGB colour and rich screens shades can shift on press.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the hex code for Blanchedalmond?

#FFEBCD — the CSS named colour “blanchedalmond”. Its RGB is (255, 235, 205).

Is Blanchedalmond good for text or backgrounds?

As a background, black text reads best on it (18.02:1). As text itself on white it reaches 1.17:1 — not enough for body text at AA; use it for headings, large text or accents.

What colours go with Blanchedalmond?

Its complement #CDE1FF for contrast, analogous #FFD2CD and #FAFFCD for calm combinations, and the 50–950 shade scale above for monochrome designs.

What is Blanchedalmond in CMYK for printing?

C0 M8 Y20 K0. CMYK conversions of screen colours are a starting point — always proof before a print run.
